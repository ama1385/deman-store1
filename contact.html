Last updated: 8 October 2026

How to reach us

  • For orders, refunds and questions: open a ticket in the server, and include the transaction ID if you have an order.
  • We reply as soon as we can. Please do not open more than one ticket for the same order.
  • For quick questions you can ask the support assistant from the chat button at the bottom of the page.
